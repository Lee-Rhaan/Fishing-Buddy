03 / COASTAL EXPLORER

Find your water.

Explore recognised fishing areas, then check access and safety on arrival.

Choose water by structure

SANDY GULLIES

Look for darker channels and moving water. Kob and steenbras may forage in surf-zone gullies.

ROCK & REEF

Structure and kelp can attract blacktail and hottentot. Avoid exposed ledges in heavy swell.

ESTUARY MOUTHS

Potential feeding areas but may have protected zones and special restrictions. Check signage before fishing.

04 / THE TACKLE ROOM

Build the right setup.

Pick a fish. We'll show a practical shore-angling starting point.

Match the bait

Buy bait from legitimate tackle shops. Collecting shellfish, worms or redbait can require permits and may be prohibited in protected areas. Never collect without checking local rules.

START HERE

Three essential setups

🎣

Light rock & surf

Approx. 9–11 ft rod, lighter sinkers and small hooks for blacktail and other smaller reef fish. Prioritise bite sensitivity.

〰️

General surf

Approx. 12–14 ft surf rod with a suitable reel and sinker rating for kob and steenbras. Match casting weight to rod rating.

🪝

Heavy surf

More powerful surf tackle for larger fish and rougher conditions. Stronger tackle does not make exposed rock fishing safe.
