How to read the sea

01
Wind

Strong onshore wind can make casting harder and build messy surf; offshore wind may flatten nearshore chop but can create other hazards.

02
Swell

Long-period swell can send powerful sets onto rocks even when the sea looks calm between waves. Wave models do not predict individual breakers.

03
Light and tide

Dawn, dusk and changing tides can be productive for some species, depending on habitat and local conditions. Use these as clues, not guarantees.
